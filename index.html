About me

Research rooted in the field. Insights backed by data.

I am an applied agronomist and plant scientist with a Ph.D. in Biological Sciences (Plant Science) and experience studying crop production, soil fertility, nutrient management, and soil–water–plant relationships across the Great Plains.

My work brings together multi-location field experiments, soil and plant measurements, environmental observations, and statistical analysis to address real-world crop management questions. I also enjoy communicating research through field days, demonstrations, and collaboration with producers and interdisciplinary teams.
